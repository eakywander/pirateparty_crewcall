PHOTO BOOTH STRIP BORDERS  (optional, for booth.html)
=====================================================
Design at 1200 x 3600 px (2 x 6 in). Use strip-template.png as a guide:
the 3 gray boxes are where the photos go, and they cover your art there.
PNG is best. Upload next to booth.html with these exact names:

  strip-1.png            replaces border 1 (Parchment)
  strip-2.png            replaces border 2 (Captain's gold)
  strip-3.png            replaces border 3 (High seas)

Extras (only if you want them):
  strip-1-overlay.png    art drawn ON TOP of the photos. Must be a PNG
                         with see-through holes where the photos show.
  strip-1-print.png      a black-and-white version used only for the
                         Rollo. Without it, your color border is
                         converted to black and white automatically.
  (same for -2 and -3)
